### Conventions chosen, and enforced on every row

- **Yield = trailing-12-month (TTM) distribution yield.** Not SEC 30-day yield. No SEC 30-day figure
  could be confirmed against a fetchable primary source for any of the six funds, so the whole table
  uses TTM. **One convention, all rows** — mixing the two would be exactly the silent-mixing failure
  in invariant 5.
- **Return = annualised TOTAL return, NAV basis, 10-year window** (or since-inception where the fund
  is younger — flagged per row, never substituted silently).
- **Expense ratio = net.** Where two sources disagree, the **higher** (more conservative) is used.
- Everything is stored in `data/funds.json` as a **decimal 0–1**, matching `lib/nzTax.ts`. The percent
  figures in the tables below are what the sources display.

## 3. Derivation — `priceGrowth = 10y total return − dividend yield`

Constitution §6 / invariant 5. This is the **only** place the decomposition is written down; the loader
computes it at load time from the two stored fields. **`priceGrowth` is never stored in the JSON**, so
it cannot go stale or disagree with its inputs.

| Ticker | Total return | − | TTM yield | = | priceGrowth | as decimal |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SCHD | 12.70% | − | 3.13% | = | **9.57%** | `0.127 − 0.0313 = 0.0957` |
| FDVV | 13.88% | − | 2.77% | = | **11.11%** | `0.1388 − 0.0277 = 0.1111` |
| VYMI | 11.00% | − | 3.49% | = | **7.51%** | `0.110 − 0.0349 = 0.0751` |
| DGRO | 13.40% | − | 1.89% | = | **11.51%** | `0.134 − 0.0189 = 0.1151` |
| EUFN | 14.50% | − | 3.97% | = | **10.53%** | `0.145 − 0.0397 = 0.1053` |
| VIG | 13.00% | − | 1.50% | = | **11.50%** | `0.130 − 0.0150 = 0.1150` |

These are float64 subtractions and will not be bit-exact (`0.127 − 0.0313` yields
`0.09570000000000001`). Tests must use `toBeCloseTo`, not `toBe` (invariant 15).

Note (invariant 7): these total returns are **already net of the expense ratio** — issuer NAV
performance is reported after fees. A projection must not charge `expenseRatio` again on top of
`priceGrowth`. `expenseRatio` is carried for display and for fee-model slices that swap in a gross
return, not to be silently subtracted.

## 4. What is `null`, and why

**`dividendGrowth` is `null` on all six rows.** No issuer publishes a multi-year dividend-per-share
CAGR for any of these funds. The only figures that could be fetched were **1-year** dividend growth —
SCHD 2.24% and VIG 1.75% (https://stockanalysis.com/etf/schd/dividend/ and
https://stockanalysis.com/etf/vig/dividend/, 2026-08-06) — and a trailing 1-year figure used as a
30-year assumption is precisely what Constitution §6 forbids. The previous file's values (SCHD 10.96,
FDVV 12.86, VYMI 14.40, DGRO 7.80, VIG 10.15) came from digrin.com, were self-described as "moderate
confidence", and showed a 7.0–11.5% spread across sources for SCHD alone. **They are not carried
forward.** Sourcing dividend growth properly means computing a per-share CAGR from a full
distribution history; that is its own slice.

**No `0` is used to mean "unknown" anywhere in this table** (Constitution §8). Every number present is
a real measured figure; everything else is `null`.

## 5. Conflicts found, and how each was resolved

1. **SCHD, the cross-file contradiction the PRD names.** `data/etfs.json` carried 3.30% yield,
   `data/dividend_portfolio.json` carried 3.30% (and 3.6% before its 2026-08-06 edit).
   **Resolved: 3.13%** TTM (AAII 2026-07-31), cross-checked at 3.11% (stockanalysis 2026-08-06).
   Neither old file was right.

2. **The polluted `sharePriceGrowth` column.** `data/dividend_portfolio.json` currently holds SCHD
   24.08, FDVV 19.64, VYMI 33.83, DGRO 21.00, EUFN 28.45, VIG 17.53. Its own `_meta.notes` admits
   these are **trailing 1-year TOTAL returns**. Two stacked errors — wrong measure (double-counts with
   `dividendYield`) and wrong horizon. **Every one of those six numbers is discarded.** Compare against
   §3: the correct SCHD figure is 9.57%, not 24.08% — a 2.5× overstatement of the growth assumption.

3. **DGRO 10-year return.** A search snippet attributed **11.0%** to the iShares fact sheet as of
   2026-06-30; AAII shows **13.4%** as of 2026-07-31. The 403 blocked direct verification. **13.4% is
   used**, because (a) the snippet was a search summariser's paraphrase, not a fetched document, and
   (b) 13.4% is consistent with DGRO's 12.58% since-inception over 12.2 years, a window that includes
   the weak 2014–2016 start. **This is the least certain number in the table. Re-verify it first.**

4. **EUFN expense ratio.** AAII 0.49%, stockanalysis 0.48%. **0.49% used** (higher = conservative on
   cost). A 1bp gap is far below model error but is recorded rather than hidden.

5. **VYMI expense ratio.** A search snippet mentioned 0.28%, almost certainly a stale historical
   figure. Two directly-fetched sources both show **0.07%**. 0.07% used.

6. **FDVV has no 10-year history.** Inception 2016-09-12 — 9.90 years as of 2026-08-06. The 13.88%
   figure is **since inception**, and the row must carry
   `totalReturnWindowYears: 9.9, totalReturnIsSinceInception: true`. It reaches a true 10-year window
   in September 2026. **A shorter window is never substituted silently.**

## 6. Sanity check on the set

`SCHD 12.7 < VIG 13.0 < DGRO 13.4 < FDVV 13.88 < EUFN 14.5`, with `VYMI 11.0` lowest. The ordering
matches the funds' tilts — a growth-leaning dividend fund (DGRO, FDVV) above a high-yield value fund
(SCHD), ex-US (VYMI) below US. EUFN is a single-sector European bank fund coming off a very strong
recovery decade; its since-inception figure of 7.85% over 16.5 years shows how much the window
choice drives it. **A 10-year window ending in a strong market overstates a 30-year assumption for
every fund here.** That is a modelling caveat for the UI slice, not a reason to alter the data.
