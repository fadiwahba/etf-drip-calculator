## Remediation plan — ordered by (financial impact × effort)

**Phase 1 — stop the bleeding (highest impact, days not weeks)**

1. **Build `lib/nzTax.ts` as the single tax engine.** FIF with de minimis threshold (parameterised,
   $50,000 default); FDR = 5% × opening value; CV alternative with **lower-of** selection; rate =
   PIR (capped 28%) for PIE or marginal rate for direct; US WHT credited against the FIF liability,
   capped at it; ordinary dividend taxation below the threshold; threshold crossing handled
   mid-projection. Every rate cited to IRD. **This subsumes findings 1, 5, 11.**
2. **Fix the four arithmetic defects** — findings 2, 3, 4, 6. Each is a small, surgical change with a
   hand-computed test.
3. **Add a test runner and fixtures** (finding 15) *before* 1 and 2 land, so each fix is proven by a
   failing-then-passing test rather than asserted.

**Phase 2 — make the numbers honest**

4. Geometric period rate; decouple compounding from contribution frequency (7).
5. Resolve price-only vs total-return once, and populate real yields + expense ratios in
   `data/etfs.json` (8).
6. Fix reported return metrics to exclude contributions (9).
7. Expose gross CAGR and tax rate as inputs in `RetirementAnalysis`; drive net from `lib/nzTax.ts`
   instead of a hardcoded constant (10).

**Phase 3 — completeness and hygiene**

8. FX rate + spread, brokerage/platform fees (14).
9. Input validation with surfaced errors instead of zero-coercion (13).
10. Delete the confirmed dead code (12).

## Open questions — need Fady's answer before Phase 1 can be specced

These change the tax model materially. They are not implementation details.

1. **Which platform?** (Sharesies / Hatch / IBKR / InvestNow / Tiger) — sets brokerage, FX spread,
   and whether W-8BEN is auto-filed (15% vs 30% US WHT).
2. **Direct US ETFs, or NZ PIE wrappers?** — decides PIR-capped 28% vs marginal 33%/39%. This is the
   single largest lever in the whole model.
3. **Marginal tax rate and PIR?**
4. **Is the foreign-holdings cost already over NZ$50,000?** (The $100k default inputs suggest yes,
   meaning FIF applies **now**, not at some future point.)
5. **Are the `avgReturn` figures in `etfs.json` price-only or total return?** Decides whether the
   DRIP toggle double-counts.
6. **Is `RetirementAnalysis copy.tsx` a deliberate reference copy, or deletable?**
